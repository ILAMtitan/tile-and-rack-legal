# Tile&Rack Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Tile&Rack is published by Axiom Tile Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how Tile&Rack handles information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

Tile&Rack does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or other personal information.

The app may store gameplay preferences, settings, unlock state, and similar app data locally on your device so the app can function properly.

## Purchases and Subscriptions

Tile&Rack offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payments and manages App Store purchase records. Tile&Rack receives purchase status information needed to unlock paid features, but we do not receive or store your payment card information.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Tile&Rack does not use third-party advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Tile&Rack does not currently use third-party analytics services.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information related to App Store purchases is handled by Apple according to Apple's terms and privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Tile&Rack is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Retention

Local app settings and gameplay data remain on your device unless you delete the app or clear the app's data. App Store purchase history is managed by Apple.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this location with an updated effective date.
